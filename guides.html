FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is JLSPT?

JLSPT stands for JL Streaming Project Team. It is a fan organized project focused on supporting JL through coordinated streaming campaigns, resources, and community activities.

Where should I start if I am new?

Start with Getting Started and Streaming Basics. Once you understand the general process, check the current campaign specific guide.

What is the difference between a platform guide and a campaign guide?

Platform guides explain how a specific service works. Campaign specific guides explain what participants should do for a particular JLSPT campaign.

Where can I see the current campaign?

The active campaign is shown on the JLSPT Home page and Campaigns page. Campaign specific instructions are also available in this Guides section.

Where can I check upcoming activities?

Visit the Schedule page for upcoming streaming sessions, regular activities, events, and completed activities.

Where can I ask for help?

Check the Troubleshooting guide first. Community help will also be available through JLSPT Corner as community features are introduced.
