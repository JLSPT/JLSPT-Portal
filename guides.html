🎵 Spotify Guide

Support JL and AHOF releases through Spotify.

01 · Use the Official Release

Search for the official JL or AHOF release and play the correct track.

02 · Listen Naturally

Avoid excessive looping. Follow JLSPT instructions when a focused streaming campaign is active.

03 · Check the Playlist

Use the recommended playlist or streaming setup when one is provided for a campaign.

▶ YouTube Guide

Support official music videos and JL content.

01 · Search the Official Video

When instructed, manually search for the official video instead of repeatedly using a direct link.

02 · Watch Fully

Watch the music video from beginning to end and avoid skipping through the video.

03 · Engage Naturally

Like the video and leave a genuine comment if you want to. Share the official video when possible.

📻 Stationhead Guide

Join JLSPT listening sessions and collaborative streams.

01 · Join the Station

Open the JLSPT Stationhead station and join whenever an official listening session is active.

02 · Follow the Host

Check the session instructions and playlist provided by the host or project team.

03 · Participate

Stay during the session when possible and participate according to the announced guidelines.

Streaming Basics

Simple reminders for effective and genuine participation.

✓ Use Official Sources

Prioritize official music videos, official artist pages, and legitimate streaming platforms.

✓ Keep It Genuine

Follow platform rules and avoid behavior that may look automated or artificial.

✓ Follow Campaign Instructions

Streaming methods can vary by campaign. Always check the latest JLSPT instructions.

Quick Troubleshooting

Having trouble? Check these common fixes first.

Video Not Playing

Check your internet connection, refresh the page, and make sure you are using the official video.

Song Not Appearing

Confirm that you are searching for the correct artist, title, and official release.

Stationhead Issue

Check the session announcement and reconnect to the station if necessary.
