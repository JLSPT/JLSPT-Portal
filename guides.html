COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

Answers to frequently asked questions about JLSPT and streaming projects.

What is JLSPT?

JLSPT stands for JL Streaming Project Team. It organizes streaming resources, campaigns, schedules, and guides to help supporters work toward shared streaming goals for JL and AHOF.

Where can I find active campaigns?

Active campaigns are listed on the Campaigns page. Each campaign includes its platform, goal, progress, missions, and relevant instructions.

Do I need to join every streaming activity?

No. Participate whenever you are available and follow the specific instructions for each campaign. Consistent genuine support is more important than forcing yourself to stream continuously.

Where can I find streaming schedules?

Upcoming sessions and other JLSPT activities are listed on the Schedule page. Schedule details may be updated when official information is confirmed.

What should I do if I encounter a problem?

Check the Troubleshooting section first. If the issue is still unresolved, follow the support instructions provided by the JLSPT team for the relevant campaign or platform.

Can I participate even if I am new to streaming?

Yes. Start with the Start Here guides, learn the basics, and follow the campaign instructions step by step.
