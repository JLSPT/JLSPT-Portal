NEED A HAND?

Quick Troubleshooting

The video or song won't play

Check your internet connection, refresh the app, update it if needed, and open the official link again.

The official link looks incorrect

Return to the Music & Video page and use the official link listed for that release.

Stationhead isn't playing

Check the app connection and station status. The station may be paused during an official collaboration stream.

Where can I find current missions?
