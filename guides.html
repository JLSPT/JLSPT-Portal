Streaming Basics

Important principles to remember.

Genuine Activity

Use the platform normally and interact with content genuinely. Avoid bots, automation, or artificial activity.

Follow Platform Rules

Platform policies can change. Always follow the latest official rules and recommendations.

Don't Force It

Healthy and sustainable support is better than using methods that could negatively affect an account or a campaign.
